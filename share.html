Daily reminders

Up to three reminders a day, even when Fitness is closed. Pick the ones you want, then tap Enable notifications.

On iPhone, add Fitness to your Home Screen first
  1. In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Open Fitness from the new Home Screen icon and sign in.
  3. Come back here and tap Enable notifications.

Times are Riyadh time.

Not getting notifications on Android?
  1. Open Settings → Apps → Fitness (or Chrome / Samsung Internet) → Notifications, and turn them on.
  2. Check that Do Not Disturb is off.
  3. Turn off battery saving for the app.
