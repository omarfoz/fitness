DAILY REMINDERS

Device notifications

A gentle reminder three times a day, even when Fitness is closed. Choose the reminders you want, then allow notifications on your device.

Install Fitness on your iPhone first In Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Open Fitness using the new Home Screen icon, sign in, and tap Enable notifications below.

Times are in Riyadh (Saudi Arabia), UTC+3.

Samsung/Android: if a test is accepted but nothing appears, check Android Settings → Apps → Fitness, Samsung Internet or Chrome → Notifications, then check Do Not Disturb and battery restrictions.
